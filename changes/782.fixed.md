`complete(handle_rrule=True)` on a recurring task with fixed times (`BY*` parts in the `RRULE`, e.g. `FREQ=YEARLY;BYMONTH=4,9`) did not advance the task when it was completed before its `DTSTART`: the next occurrence was looked for after the completion time, which found the occurrence just completed.  It is now looked for after `DTSTART`.
