`add_todo()`, `add_event()`, `add_object()` (and their `save_*()` aliases) and `vcal.create_ical()` given an `ical` fragment holding only a VALARM took the VALARM for the whole object: no VTODO or VEVENT was created, the properties given, the UID included, landed on the alarm, and the calendar got an object holding no task or event that no search could find.  Such a fragment now goes into the new object, like a fragment of property lines does.
