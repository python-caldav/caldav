`compatibility_hints`: several server profiles are updated from a caldav-server-tester run, so with `features: <server>` configured, `is_supported()` may give a different answer for the features named here.

- DAViCal, SOGo and Bedework 3 now state `sync-token.invalid-token`, `sync-token.limit` and `sync-token.limit.initial-sync`, and Bedework 5 the two `limit` features, where they used to inherit `fragile` from `sync-token` or default to `full`.  DAViCal and SOGo ignore `DAV:limit` and answer a never-issued sync token with a 207 and no error.  Bedework 3 refuses a token it never issued, as it should, and answers a `DAV:limit` with a 500.  Bedework 5 cuts the reply at `DAV:limit` without the 507 the RFC asks for: following the tokens loses nothing, but a client takes the cut reply as complete and gets the rest only on its next sync.
- Purelymail: `search.is-not-defined`, `search.time-range.event` and `search.time-range.todo` are `full` again (they were `fragile`), and `calendar-color` and `calendar-order` are `full`.
- Infomaniak: the wait after deleting a calendar (`synchronous-write.delete-calendar`) is raised from 12 to 20 seconds; a deleted calendar could still be there after 12.
