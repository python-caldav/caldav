`delete()` on a single occurrence of a recurring event or task, as returned by `search(..., expand=True)`, deleted the whole series.  It now takes only that occurrence out of the series: it is added to the `EXDATE` of the master, and an override of it is dropped (one covering this and the later occurrences, `RANGE=THISANDFUTURE`, moves on to the next occurrence).  For an invitation to some occurrences only (no master), the override is removed, and the object deleted when it was the last one.  `delete(only_this_recurrence=False)` deletes the whole series as before.
