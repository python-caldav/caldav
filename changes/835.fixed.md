An href is decoded once.  A UID holding a `/` is stored as `%252F` (the name `save()` gives it, and what iCloud lists), and a second decode turned that into a `/`: `search()`, `children()` and sync results for a UID that is a URL pointed at a URL that named nothing, and `multiget()` returned any object with a `/` in its UID under a URL that does not exist.
