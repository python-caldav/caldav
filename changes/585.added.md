Compatibility features `save-load.todo.uncomplete` (a completed task can be made pending again with `Todo.uncomplete()`; GMX keeps it completed) and `search.time-range.todo.no-dtstart.no-due` (a date search finds a task with neither DTSTART nor DUE, as RFC 4791 section 9.9 says it should; Zimbra does not).  They replace the last two old-style flags in the Zimbra and GMX profiles.
