`complete(handle_rrule=True, rrule_mode="thisandfuture")` treated every component of the object as an instance of the task, the first as the series and the last as the one to complete.  So it raised `KeyError: 'RRULE'` for a task with its `VTIMEZONE` before the `VTODO`, the usual order in tasks from other clients and after reading `vobject_instance`, and completed a `VTIMEZONE` that came after it.  It now skips `VTIMEZONE` components.
